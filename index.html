Who we are

Macro Buddy is a nutrition tracking app published on Google Play by Masiot Apps, an independent developer. Contact: juozasj375@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We have no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Purchases, if any, are handled entirely by Google Play.
  • Your profile, food log and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • Only two things ever leave your phone: a barcode number (to look up a product) and a photo of a nutrition label (to read the text) — and only when you choose to scan.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your device: your profile (age, sex, height, weight, goal and activity level), your daily food log, your settings and achievements, and a cache of products you have scanned. This data never leaves your device, we cannot see it, and we do not sell or share it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently. You can also clear the scan cache in Settings.

Camera

The camera is used only while you have the scanner open, to read barcodes and to take a photo of a nutrition label. Photos are not saved to your gallery.

Third-party services

  • Open Food Facts (Open Food Facts association, France) — when you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to the Open Food Facts database to find the product's nutrition values. No personal data is sent. Their privacy policy.
  • OCR.space (a9t9 software GmbH, Germany) — when you photograph a nutrition label, a reduced copy of that photo is sent to OCR.space to recognise the text. Please point the camera only at the label. Their privacy policy.
  • Google Play — installs and updates the app under Google's privacy policy.

All connections use encrypted HTTPS.

Health information

Macro Buddy gives informational recommendations only. It is not medical, dietary or health advice and does not replace a doctor or dietitian.

Age

The app is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete — all of it is under your control on your device. If you have any question about privacy, write to us at the address above.

  • Nutrition values come from public databases, label scans and estimates and may contain errors. Always check the package if accuracy matters to you, for example for allergies or a medical diet.
